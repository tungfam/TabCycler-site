LESS SEARCHING. MORE DOING.

One shortcut. Back in the flow.

  1. 01

    Press your shortcut

    Start with ⌘ ⇧ Y. Prefer another combination? Change it in Chrome’s extension shortcut settings.

  2. 02

    Hold to browse

    Keep Command held for 200 ms to see up to eight recent tabs. Use Left/Right or press your shortcut again to move the highlight.

  3. 03

    Release to switch

    Release Command to activate the highlighted tab. Just want the previous tab? A quick tap switches without showing the cycler.

A SMALL TOOL. A SMALL FOOTPRINT.

Your recent-tab order stays in Chrome.

No backend, accounts, analytics, or advertising. Tab information is processed locally, with recent-tab IDs kept in Chrome’s temporary session storage.

Read the privacy policy ↗

A couple of things to know

Made for Chrome on macOS. Windows and Linux support are planned. Start the shortcut while a normal webpage has focus: Chrome’s address bar, internal pages, Chrome Web Store, and some embedded frames have keyboard or extension restrictions. Enter is available as a selection fallback.
